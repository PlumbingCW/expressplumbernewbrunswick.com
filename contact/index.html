24/7 Emergency Line Open

Contact New Brunswick Express Plumbing

The way to reach a plumber right now is to call. The line is open 24 hours a day, every day, for emergencies and planned work.

(732) 466-1098

Call the dispatch line

Call (732) 466-1098 and a dispatcher will ask for your address, what is happening and whether water is still running. If water is spreading, shut off the main water valve first, then call.

We do not use web forms or chat. A phone call gets the problem described once and a plumber lined up, without waiting for an inbox to be checked.

What to have ready

Photos are helpful but not required. If a ceiling is dripping, place a bucket under it and keep people away from any wet light fixture.

  • The street address, including the unit number for an apartment or multifamily building.
  • A short description of what you see, hear or smell, and when it started.
  • Whether you have already shut off the main water valve or the gas to a water heater.
  • The best number to reach you, and whether someone can let the plumber in.

Email and mailing address

For questions that are not urgent, email contact@expressplumbernewbrunswick.com. Replies to email are not immediate, so call for anything that cannot wait.

Our address is 303 George St, New Brunswick, NJ 08901. Plumbers work from vehicles and travel to your address, so please call before you visit. The map below shows the address.

Where we send plumbers

We dispatch within New Brunswick and to the nearby Middlesex County towns of Edison, Piscataway, East Brunswick, North Brunswick and Highland Park.

If you are outside those areas, call anyway and the dispatcher will tell you whether a plumber can reach you. You can also browse the full list of plumbing services before you call.
