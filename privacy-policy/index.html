Privacy Policy

This page explains what information we handle when you visit this website or call our line, and what choices you have.

(732) 466-1098

Who we are

This website is operated by New Brunswick Express Plumbing, 303 George St, New Brunswick, NJ 08901. You can reach us by phone at (732) 466-1098 or by email at contact@expressplumbernewbrunswick.com.

This policy covers expressplumbernewbrunswick.com and the phone line shown on it. It does not cover other websites that we link to or that appear inside an embedded map.

Information we receive when you call

Our site has no contact forms. The main way we receive information from you is by phone. When you call, we receive your phone number and whatever you choose to tell the dispatcher, such as your address, the plumbing problem and the best time to reach you.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call and ask to be handled another way, or hang up and contact us by email instead.

We use call information to dispatch a plumber, to give you a price before work starts, to follow up on the visit and to improve how we answer the phone.

Information we receive when you email us

If you send an email, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use it only to reply to you and to keep a record of the conversation.

Website data and cookies

This website does not ask you to create an account and does not use advertising cookies of its own. Like most web servers, our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability.

Pages that show a map load a Google Maps embed. Google may set its own cookies or collect usage data when that map loads. Google's use of that data is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

How we share information

We share call details with the licensed plumber or contractor who is sent to your address, because they need them to do the job. We may also share information with service providers who help us run the phone line and website, and when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep call records and emails for as long as we need them to handle your service request, resolve disputes and meet legal or accounting duties. After that we delete them or remove identifying details.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it by emailing contact@expressplumbernewbrunswick.com. We will respond as required by the law that applies to you.

If you receive a call or text from us that you do not want, tell us and we will stop contacting you about that matter.

Children

This website is meant for adults who own, rent or manage property. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version on this page. Please check back if you want to see the current wording.

Questions

Questions about this policy can go to contact@expressplumbernewbrunswick.com or to (732) 466-1098.

Our service area

We dispatch across New Brunswick and the nearby Middlesex County towns of Edison, Piscataway, East Brunswick, North Brunswick and Highland Park.
