Plumbing Services in New Brunswick, NJ

Twenty-one plumbing services, grouped by the problem you are trying to solve. Call any time to talk through which one fits.

(732) 466-1098

Find the right plumbing service

Pick the group that matches what you see. Drain and sewer problems show up as slow water, gurgling or smells. Water heater problems show up as cold showers, leaks at the base or odd noises. Leaks and pipe problems show up as wet spots, high bills or a loud burst.

Fixture work covers the toilets, faucets and disposals you touch every day, and basement and property work covers sump pumps, waterproofing, boilers and commercial buildings. If you are unsure, call the line and describe the symptom. A dispatcher will point you to the right service and schedule the visit.

Drains and Sewer

01Drain Cleaning

Clogged sinks, tubs, toilets and main drains cleared and tested.

02Hydro Jetting

High-pressure water scouring for grease, scale and root-packed lines.

03Sewer Line Cleaning

Main sewer backups cleared from the cleanout to the street.

04Sewer Camera Inspection

A recorded camera run that shows what is wrong underground.

05Sewer Line Repair

Spot repairs, pipe lining and replacement for failed sewer lines.

Water Heaters

06Water Heater Repair

No hot water, leaks and strange noises diagnosed on the spot.

07Water Heater Replacement

Failed tanks swapped out, hauled away and brought up to code.

08Water Heater Installation

Planned tank installs sized for the home, vented and permitted correctly.

09Tankless Water Heater

Installation, repair and descaling of on-demand water heaters.

Leaks and Pipes

10Leak Detection

Running meters, high bills and wet walls traced to the source.

11Burst Pipe Repair

Shutoff, repair and drying guidance when a pipe lets go.

12Water Line Repair

Repair and replacement of the pipe from the street to your meter.

13Repiping

Whole-house repiping for old galvanized, lead-era and failing pipe.

14Plumbing Installation

New fixtures, lines and rough-in work for remodels and additions.

Fixtures

15Toilet Repair

17Garbage Disposal Repair

Basement and Property

18Sump Pump Repair and Installation

19Basement Waterproofing

Interior drainage and water control for wet basements.

20Boiler Repair
