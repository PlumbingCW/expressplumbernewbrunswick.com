About New Brunswick Express Plumbing

We answer plumbing calls day and night in New Brunswick and nearby Middlesex County towns, and send a licensed and insured plumber to the address.

(732) 466-1098

What we do

New Brunswick Express Plumbing is a 24/7 plumbing dispatch service. You call (732) 466-1098, describe the problem, and we arrange a licensed and insured plumber for your address.

The work covers drain cleaning, water heaters, leak detection, sewer lines, fixtures and the rest of the services listed on our services page. Emergencies and planned jobs both go through the same phone line.

Who this is for

Homeowners with a leak they cannot find, landlords with a tenant calling at night, and shop owners with a floor drain that will not clear all call the same line. The common thread is that waiting costs money, and a clear conversation early saves it.

The business is based at 303 George St in New Brunswick and works across the nearby towns. It is a phone-first operation. There is no online booking and no form to fill out, because a real conversation is faster for almost every plumbing problem.

How a call works

  1. You callA dispatcher asks for the address, the symptoms and whether water is still running, and gives you shutoff steps if damage is spreading.
  2. We line up a plumberWe look for the soonest plumber who can reach you. Same-day appointments are offered when available, and we do not promise a fixed arrival time.
  3. You get a clear priceThe plumber looks at the problem and gives a clear price before work starts. Nothing proceeds without your approval.
  4. The work is done and checkedThe plumber completes the repair, tests it and explains what was done and what to watch for.

How we decide what to recommend

A plumber who profits from every upsell is hard to trust, so the approach here is simple. Diagnose first, explain what is wrong in plain words, and give a price before any work begins. If a cheaper fix exists and it will last, it gets mentioned first.

Some problems are not worth repairing. A water heater that has failed at the tank, or a pipe that has leaked at several joints, is usually better replaced. Others look dramatic and turn out to be small. A phone call is often enough to tell the difference, and a dispatcher will say so rather than book a visit you do not need.

What we stand behind

Licensed and insured

Plumbers sent to your home are licensed and insured. You can ask any plumber for proof of a license and check it with the state licensing board.

Clear price first

You hear the price before work starts, and any change on site is explained and approved before it goes ahead.

Straight advice

If a repair is not worth the money, the plumber will say so. A failing 15 year old tank, for example, is often better replaced than patched.

What a plumber needs from you

The best repairs start with a good description. Say what you see, what you hear, and when it started. Say whether the problem is in one fixture or everywhere. Those details narrow the cause before anyone arrives.

If you can, shut off the water at the main valve and keep the area clear. Move anything valuable out of the way, and keep pets away from open work. A clear path to the problem saves time and keeps the price lower.

Where we work

Our address is 303 George St in New Brunswick. We send plumbers across the city and to Edison, Piscataway, East Brunswick, North Brunswick and Highland Park.

What we do not do

We do not take bookings through forms or chat, and we do not quote a price over the phone for a problem nobody has seen. Many plumbing problems look alike from the description, so the plumber confirms the cause on site before pricing the repair.

If a problem involves a gas smell, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before calling us.

Frequently asked questions

We cover the services listed on the services page, from drain cleaning and water heaters to leak detection and commercial work. If a job falls outside that list, the dispatcher will tell you.
