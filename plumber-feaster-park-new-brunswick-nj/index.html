Finding Feaster Park

Feaster Park is in New Brunswick's Second Ward. The park and neighboring Pittman Park sit between Commercial Avenue and Throop Avenue, separated by Handy Street. The City, the Trust for Public Land and Rutgers CUES planned a redesign of the two parks in 2018 and 2019.

Official neighborhood boundaries are not published in the sources we checked, so we describe the area by those anchor streets. If your street is close but not named here, say so on the call. A nearby address is fine for a repair.

Source: Rutgers CUES, Feaster Park project page

A mix of house types and ages

Property listings in the area show single-family and two-family houses, townhouses and at least one condo, on streets such as Throop Avenue, Jones Avenue and North Talmadge Street. Listed construction years range from 1920 to 2005, so the plumbing behind the walls is not one age.

That mix is why a phone description helps. A 1920s two-family and a 2005 townhouse can need different parts for the same symptom.

Two floors, one problem

Two-family houses often share a drain stack, and sometimes a water heater or main shutoff. A backup in the first-floor tub can start with a clog serving the second floor. A sewer line cleaning call is the usual next step when more than one fixture is slow.

If water is coming through a ceiling, shut off the water to the upstairs unit if you can reach a valve, and call. Do not stand in water near outlets.

Hot water in smaller homes

Smaller units tend to have one water heater working hard for a full household. When it leaks at the base or stops heating, the choice is between water heater repair and a swap. Ask the plumber to explain which one fits your tank and why before agreeing.

If the tank is in a shared basement, find out who owns it before you call.

Tenants, owners and repair requests

Citywide, about 21 percent of New Brunswick housing units are owner-occupied. A data aggregator also reports that most homes in Feaster Park are renter occupied, though we treat that as an estimate. Either way, many calls here involve a tenant and an owner.

Tell the landlord in writing, keep photos, and let us know on the phone if approval is pending. We will explain what the repair involves so you can pass it on.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts, New Brunswick city, ACS 2020 to 2024
