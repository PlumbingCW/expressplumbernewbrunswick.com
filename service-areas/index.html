Towns we serve around New Brunswick

The business is based at 303 George St in New Brunswick, and the same dispatch line covers five neighboring towns in Middlesex County. Each town has its own page with the ZIP codes we serve there, the services people call about, and what to do before a plumber arrives.

If your town is not listed, call anyway. Nearby addresses are often within reach, and a dispatcher can tell you quickly whether a same-day appointment is possible.

ZIP codes by town

Each town page lists the ZIP codes it covers. Edison has the most because the township uses several. The list below is a quick reference, and the individual pages explain how those codes map to addresses.

If you are not sure which ZIP applies to you, read it from your mail or your water bill. The dispatcher will confirm the address when you call, so a wrong guess costs nothing.

  • Edison: 08817, 08820 and 08837
  • Piscataway: 08854
  • East Brunswick: 08816
  • North Brunswick: 08902
  • Highland Park: 08904
  • New Brunswick: 08901 and 08903

What to have ready when you call

A few details shorten the call and help the plumber arrive with the right parts. None of them need to be exact. A rough description is enough to start.

If you are renting, mention it. A landlord or property manager often has to approve work, and a plumber can explain what to ask them while the water is stopped.

  1. The addressStreet, town and ZIP, plus any apartment or unit number and how to get into the building.
  2. What you seeWhere the water is, which fixtures are affected, and whether it is clear or dirty.
  3. What you have doneSay whether the main valve is closed and whether you have stopped using the drains.
  4. How to reach youA phone that will be answered. A dispatcher may call back with a question or an arrival update.

Why the towns share one line

Emergency plumbing does not follow municipal borders. A burst pipe in Highland Park and a backed-up main in North Brunswick are the same kind of call, and one dispatch line lets the schedule move to wherever the need is.

The trade-off is that timing depends on the day. Same-day appointments are offered when they are available, and a dispatcher will tell you honestly when the next opening is. Nothing is promised that the schedule cannot deliver.

If your town is not on the list

Service areas are not fences. Neighboring towns such as Franklin Township, Somerset, South River and Old Bridge sit close to the towns above, and a dispatcher can tell you quickly whether a visit is practical for your address. The honest answer sometimes depends on the schedule that day.

When a call is outside what the schedule can cover, you will hear that on the phone rather than after a long wait. That is better than a promise that cannot be kept, and it lets you call another plumber without losing time.
